HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN VIDEOS
==========================
Drop your `.mp4` video clips into this folder (`public/videos/`) using these filenames:

- memory1.mp4
- memory2.mp4
- memory3.mp4

Alternatively, paste an external `.mp4` URL into `externalUrl` inside `src/config/birthdayConfig.ts`.
Keep videos under ~25 MB and H.264 encoded so they load smoothly on smartphones!
